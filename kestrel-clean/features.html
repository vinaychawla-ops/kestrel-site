How an alert works

01 / DETECT

Sub-Second Anomaly Trigger

Kernel-level raw socket detects 3 consecutive dropped ICMP echoes or failed HTTP 200 health check assertions.

probe_drop: 3/3 @ 192.168.1.1
02 / NOTIFY

Zero-Relay Push Dispatch

Direct local webhook or encrypted priority notification sent straight to your phone, bypassing third-party cloud queues.

dispatch -> Pushover [Priority: 2]
03 / RESOLVE

Automated Heuristic Logging

Incident duration, switch port jitter, and service recovery time are committed to local WAL storage with instant recovery ping.
