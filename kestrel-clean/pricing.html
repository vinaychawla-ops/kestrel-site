Frequently Asked Questions

Is the free tier really free? expand_more
Yes. Hobby is free forever: 25 devices, 1-minute checks, and community support. No credit card, no trial countdown, no telemetry paywall — the free tier runs the same local engine as paid plans.
Does Kestrel phone home? expand_more
No. Kestrel is self-hosted, so every check, device record, and alert stays on your own network. The only outbound connection is an optional update check, which you can disable entirely.
Which platforms are supported? expand_more
Anywhere Docker runs, plus a single static binary for Linux (amd64 and arm64) — one ~15MB file, SQLite under the hood, no dependencies to babysit.
Can I migrate from another monitoring tool? expand_more
Yes. Import your devices over CSV or the REST API, and bring your check intervals with them. Most homelabs are fully migrated in an afternoon.
How do push notifications work? expand_more
The moment a check fails, Kestrel pushes an alert through your chosen provider — ntfy, Gotify, Pushover, or plain webhooks — usually before anyone in the house notices anything is wrong.
